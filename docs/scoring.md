# Scoring

Reaction cost is a step penalty plus a centralized evidence-class uncertainty penalty and confidence penalty. AND costs sum the selected reaction and every required precursor subtree. They never take the cheapest precursor. Route ranking is deterministic and tie-breaks on a canonical reaction-set signature.

Future objective profiles may add material price, availability, selectivity, stereochemical burden, protecting groups, rarity, and sustainability. Such weights belong in a validated versioned configuration, not planner branches. Heuristic estimates guide selection only and are never displayed as experimental evidence.
